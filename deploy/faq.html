Questions about our catering

Are you a family-run caterer?

Yes. The Reach Catering is a family-run Mediterranean hospitality and catering business, with roots in hospitality dating back to 2002.

Where are you based?

We are based in Hammersmith, in the Fulham Reach area of London, at Goldhurst House, Parr's Way, London W6 9AN. We cater events in Hammersmith, Fulham and across London.

What kinds of events do you cater?

We cater corporate events and conferences, weddings, private celebrations and bespoke occasions, from intimate gatherings to large-scale events.

Do you cater for corporate events and meetings?

Yes. Our menus include Breakfast & Meetings, Working Lunches & Sharing Feasts, Canapés & Working Receptions, and Corporate Dinners & Full Service Events, catered to your schedule and guest numbers.

Do you cater for weddings and private parties?

Yes. We cater weddings, birthdays, anniversaries and family gatherings, from canapé receptions and relaxed sharing feasts to seated dining.

What menus do you offer?

We offer four menus: Breakfast & Meetings, Working Lunches & Sharing Feasts, Canapés & Working Receptions, and Corporate Dinners & Full Service Events. Each can be tailored to your event.

What is Mediterranean catering?

Our food is inspired by Mediterranean and international flavours, made with fresh ingredients and beautifully presented, from elegant canapés and sharing platters to buffets and seated dining.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes. We cater to all needs: every menu can be tailored to your guest numbers, dietary requirements, style of service and budget.

Can you create a bespoke menu?

Yes. Our chefs create bespoke menus around your occasion, your guests and your preferences, inspired by Mediterranean and international flavours.

How many guests can you cater for?

We cater for events of all sizes, from intimate gatherings to large-scale events. Tell us your guest numbers and we will tailor the menu and service to suit.

Do you provide staff, equipment and tableware?

Yes. Our experienced chefs, hospitality staff and event supervisors look after your event from preparation to the final service, and we can coordinate equipment, tableware, staffing and service arrangements for you.

How do I get a price?

Send us a request with a few details about your event. We reply within one working day with a personalised catering proposal, and you can enquire for pricing on any of our menus.

How quickly will you reply?

We reply within one working day.

Still have a question?

Tell us about your event and we will reply within one working day with a personalised catering proposal.
