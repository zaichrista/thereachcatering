About Us

With a passion for exceptional food and hospitality, The Reach Catering brings together over two decades of experience in the hospitality industry, with roots dating back to 2002.

From corporate events and business gatherings to weddings, private celebrations and special occasions, we believe every event deserves delicious food, thoughtful presentation and outstanding service.

Our experienced team takes pride in creating memorable catering experiences, combining quality ingredients, beautifully prepared dishes and a personal approach to every occasion.

Whether you're hosting an intimate gathering or a large-scale event, we are here to make every moment special.

Our Catering Menus

Thoughtfully Created for Every Occasion

From intimate gatherings to large-scale corporate events, our catering menus bring together fresh ingredients, international flavours and beautiful presentation.

Whether you're planning a business conference, wedding, private celebration or networking reception, our experienced team will create a menu tailored to your occasion, preferences and dietary requirements.

Small Bites, Big Impressions

Perfect for cocktail receptions, networking events and celebrations. Discover an inviting selection of elegant canapés, creative finger food and beautifully presented bowl dishes, designed for effortless social dining.

Generous, Relaxed & Full of Flavour

From Mediterranean-inspired spreads to international favourites, our buffets and sharing menus bring people together over delicious food. Ideal for corporate lunches, family celebrations and larger gatherings, with flexible options to suit your event.

Beautifully Served, Thoughtfully Prepared

For occasions that call for a more traditional dining experience, we offer bespoke two- and three-course menus. From elegant starters to carefully prepared main courses and indulgent desserts, every detail is designed around your event.

The Perfect Start to Every Event

Keep your guests refreshed and energised throughout the day with our breakfast selections, working lunches, tea and coffee services, and conference refreshments. From morning meetings to full-day events, we provide reliable catering tailored to your schedule.

Raise a Glass to Every Occasion

Complete your event with a selection of welcome drinks, wines, beers, soft drinks and bespoke beverage packages. Our team can arrange professional bar service and drinks receptions to complement your catering experience.

Every Event Is Unique

All our menus can be tailored to suit your guest numbers, dietary requirements, style of service and budget.

Contact our team to discuss your event and receive a personalised catering proposal.
